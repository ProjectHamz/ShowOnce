    How it works

    Three steps, three moods: olive while you teach, burgundy while it watches.

    1

    Teach

    Do the procedure once. Gemini 4 Neon turns the video into a list of steps you can rename.

    Recording 0:24
    1. Open the bag
    2. Put on gloves
    3. Put glasses in bag
    4. Put tape and charger in bag
    5. Put water bottle in bag and zip
    2

    Watch

    Every 3 seconds Gemini Decision 1 looks at the last 6 seconds and answers yes or no for each step.

    The window slides one clip at a time, so no step falls between clips.

    3

    Catch

    A step counts when it is 0.85 sure once or 0.60 sure twice. Mistakes are said out loud right away.

    Extra step
    Extra step: put a phone in the bag.

    What it checks

    Steps against the procedure. Never the person.

    Skipped step

    A later step is done while an earlier one was never seen.

    Out of order

    Two steps swapped. It still counts the late step when it happens.

    Extra step

    An action that isn't in the procedure, named by Gemini 4 Neon.

    Step undone

    A finished step reversed, like an item taken back out.

    No one in view

    It pauses its judgment when nobody is there.

    Still or too dark

    Motion is measured on the device. Still clips are never sent.

    Aa

    Accessibility

    Built so people who can't watch a screen, or can't read small text, get the same help.

    • Spoken alerts in a natural voiceGemini 3.8 Flash TTS, prepared before the run so it speaks instantly.
    • Say each stepAn option to hear every finished step, not only mistakes.
    • Screen reader announcementsEvery step and alert is announced. No WCAG A or AA issues in our axe scan.
    • Larger text, light and dark, CalmBigger type, both themes, and a switch that stops all motion.
    • Alerts you can feelPhones vibrate with each alert. Alerts stay up until dismissed or 12 seconds pass.

    Under the hood

    Each Gemini model has one job.

    Gemini Decision 1

    Yes or no for each step, every 3 seconds, on a 6-second window. About 0.5 s per check.

    Gemini 4 Neon

    Turns the teaching video into steps, and names extra actions.

    Gemini 4 Carbon

    Writes the alert sentence for each mistake.

    Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS

    Speaks the alerts in a natural voice.
